# Creative Chakras

Studio site for **Creative Chakras** — a full-spectrum creative studio: branding,
graphic design, motion, video editing, social, content production, packaging, web.

**Live:** https://ajmalhasnain-ship-it.github.io/creative-chakras/

Single self-contained `index.html` — no build step, no dependencies. Type is loaded
from Google Fonts (Bricolage Grotesque / Archivo / Martian Mono / Baloo 2); everything
else is inline SVG and vanilla JS.

## The seven chakras

The studio's disciplines are mapped onto the traditional seven-chakra system, and the
mandala is drawn from that data rather than decorated to look like it — petal count,
inner yantra, colour and element all come from the tradition:

| # | Chakra | Petals | Yantra | Element | Discipline |
|---|--------|-------:|--------|---------|------------|
| 1 | Muladhara    | 4    | square + inverted triangle | earth | brand identity |
| 2 | Svadhisthana | 6    | crescent                   | water | graphic design |
| 3 | Manipura     | 10   | inverted triangle          | fire  | packaging & print |
| 4 | Anahata      | 12   | six-pointed star           | air   | social media |
| 5 | Vishuddha    | 16   | triangle + circle          | ether | film & video |
| 6 | Ajna         | 2    | triangle + Om              | light | web & interface |
| 7 | Sahasrara    | 1000 | circle                     | consciousness | motion design |

Sahasrara's thousand petals are drawn as 24, the usual convention.
